# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Report

## Objective
Analyze e-commerce transactions to understand revenue, profitability, customers, products, categories, payment behavior, and regional performance.

## Business Questions
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which categories are most profitable?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which states generate the most sales?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- What payment methods are most frequently used?
- What is the average order value?
- What proportion of orders are returned or cancelled?

## Methodology
1. Loaded raw transactional data.
2. Standardized column names and data types.
3. Removed duplicate and invalid records.
4. Created profit margin, year, month, and customer segment features.
5. Performed exploratory data analysis.
6. Used SQL for business questions.
7. Prepared Power BI-ready CSV outputs.
8. Built dashboard KPIs and visualizations.

## Key Metrics
The final values are generated automatically in `outputs/kpi_summary.csv`.

## Recommended Business Insights
Use the generated tables and charts to identify:
- revenue trends and seasonal patterns,
- high-performing categories,
- high-value products,
- high-revenue states,
- valuable customer segments,
- payment-method preferences,
- cancellation and return patterns.

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and intended for portfolio/internship demonstration. It should not be represented as proprietary company data.
